Problem sets are designed for you to test your own conceptual understanding of the material. They provide a skeleton of relevant information from lecture and workshop for you to use as a reference when you are completing homeworks and exams, which are more formal presentations of your understanding.

Problems

1. Categorizing variables

For each of the following variables, name i) what kind of variable it is and ii) why.

  1. Date for a study takes place over 3 days (11 November, 15 December, 13 January) with one observation of frog count per day

  2. Time for a study that takes place over 24 hours with one measurement of water temperature every 15 minutes (e.g. 04:15, 06:30, 18:45)

  3. Scale of 1-5 in whole numbers where 1 is strongly disagree, 3 is neither agree nor disagree, and 5 is strongly agree

  4. Count of whale sharks per day along migratory route in Baja California, Mexico (e.g. 4 whale sharks per day, 10 whale sharks per day, 0 whale sharks per day)

  5. Category of elevation gain on a hike: high, medium, or no elevation gain

  6. Year for a study that takes place over 32 years, where researchers estimate giant kelp biomass once a year (e.g. 1993, 2001, 2022)

  7. Height of an oak tree, measured in meters to the nearest whole meter (e.g. 1 m, 4 m, 25 m)

2. Central tendency and spread

  1. What units would you use for:
  • Mean
  • Median
  • Mode
  • Standard deviation
  • Variance
  • Range
  1. What functions could you use to calculate:
  • Mean
  • Median
  • Mode
  • Standard deviation
  • Variance
  • Range

3. Data visualization

  1. What is on the x-axis of a histogram? What is on the y-axis of a histogram?

  2. What is on the x-axis of a timeseries? What is on the y-axis of a timeseries?

  3. Label each component of this boxplot with what it represents mathematically.

  1. If you wanted to compare median vegetation height (cm) across 1) burned grasslands, 2) unburned grasslands, and 3) shrublands, what kind of figure would you create and why?

  2. If you wanted to understand the relationship between soil salinity (measured in mS/cm) and nitrogen concentration (measured in parts per million), what kind of figure would you create and why?

  3. If you wanted to see how bird species richness (i.e. species number) changed through time, what kind of figure would you create and why?
